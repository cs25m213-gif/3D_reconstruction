What the numbers measure.

This page preserves the distinctions between depth prediction, frame utility, reconstruction agreement and runtime. The project showcase presents measured improvements alongside the strongest relevant controls.

Depth learning

Recorded AMvalley validation AbsRel: corrected targets 3.07524%, expanded UAVScenes 2.49597%, auxiliary data 2.20894%. The relative decrease from the first to the final value is 28.2%. These share the qualified sparse-anchor-fit protocol, but initialization, update counts and data changed together. The cached DA3 comparison is 2.1125%.

Source: more-data training report, 4 November 2026. This is not a controlled data-only ablation or a mesh accuracy metric.

Reconstruction-aware selection

On 100 validation variants from 20 UAVScenes clips (clean, motion blur, defocus, mixed degradation, frame gaps), the expanded epoch-31 checkpoint scores F=0.3131257, compared with uniform 0.2331967 and DINO-middle 0.3405975 at its selected-frame counts. Relative improvement over uniform is 34.3%; wins occur in 77% of variants. Average retained fraction is 6.54%.

The earlier checkpoint scores 0.3342486 with uniform 0.2883868 and DINO-middle 0.3340183 at a different retained fraction, 14.84%. The expanded model is not the best method in that comparison. These are reference-pose TSDF experiments with predicted depth, not unrestricted live-video mesh trials. Variants are correlated through their base clips; thresholds use AMtown03. AMtown03 AUC is not an untouched-test result.

Sources: selector reconstruction evaluation and row evaluation, 16 November 2026. An earlier causal cascade experiment also failed: cheap online F=0.382457, cascade 0.342676, deep online 0.325584 under a different protocol. These scores must not be pooled with the later validation curve.

Campus demonstration

Native 4K stereo alone took 4059.675 s (67.66 min). Dense-run reports with cached sparse poses record 515.073 s for the DA3-assisted path, 864.485 s for the 84-view student-plus-fallback path and 497.741 s for selected 17-view reconstruction plus fallback. These exclude other work, including frame selection and final texturing. No complete-pipeline speedup is calculated between these differing scopes.

At 2% of robust reference scene extent, surface F is 0.953295 for DA3-84, 0.856403 for student-84, 0.795846 for selected-17, and 0.829207 for DINO-17. The reference is reconstructed 4K fused geometry. Evaluation uses common camera coordinates and a reference-bounded spatial crop. It is neither independent measured ground truth nor a measure of missing geometry outside that crop.

The interactive model is a reduced, textured web derivative of the patched campus mesh. Existing lower-resolution patches supplement gaps; they are not new high-resolution measured surfaces. Texture compression and browser rendering improve delivery, not geometry accuracy. Original reconstruction artifacts remain preserved.

Data and architecture

The union contains 149,637 admitted RGB images across nine families. The queue records final utility-shard inclusion for 12,586 shards, ending at cumulative epoch 33 / step 256,877. Admission and completion markers do not prove real-scene calibration or generalization. Qualified LiDAR, sparse SfM proxies and teacher predictions remain distinct sources.

The trained selector uses a ResNet18 RGB encoder with 384-dimensional projection and a four-layer Transformer with ADD/SWAP heads. It receives 36-dimensional state and candidate/occupant information. DINO is auxiliary supervision and a grouping/control feature. The depth backend is held fixed within a gain-label round. Alternating depth updates and refreshed utility labels, a newer common-map head, and full differentiable mesh training are separate integration goals.
